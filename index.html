Kinematic Viscosity Ratio

Thermal shearing in crankpin journals shears down polymer Viscosity Index Improvers, while sustained high-temperature oxidation polymerizes hydrocarbons into sludge. We compare actual cSt against SAE baseline specs.

OPTICAL ANALYSIS

Light Penetration & Soot

Sub-micron carbon soot, nitration polymers, and blow-by particulates absorb light photons. Optical transmittance decays non-linearly, giving an exact photometric assessment of particulate loading.

T = T_0 · e^(-α · C_soot)

Remaining Useful Life (RUL)

Our formula synthesizes consistency deviation and optical soot saturation with base oil chemistry and engine duty cycle to predict remaining kilometers before boundary metal friction occurs.

RUL_km = D_max · M_duty · [(OHI - 25) / 75]
OPTICAL BENCHMARK

DEGRADATION SPECTRUM

The 5 stages of fluid degradation visual reference for dipstick inspection and fluid condition checks.

Fresh Induction

85% - 100% Light Transmittance

Translucent golden honey. Full additive reserve, zero soot suspension, optimal boundary protection.

Mid-Life Stable

60% - 84% Light Transmittance

Warm caramel amber. Normal thermal coloration; active detergent pack actively suspending sub-micron carbon.

Degraded Amber

35% - 59% Light Transmittance

Dark roasted brown. Antioxidants partially depleted; viscosity shifts begin from high-shear mechanical stress.

Soot Saturated

15% - 34% Light Transmittance

Deep black with opaque edges. Common in direct-injection diesels; dispersants reaching threshold capacity.

Critical Sludge

< 15% Light Transmittance

Pitch black, viscous sludge. Severe risk of ring sticking, filter bypass valve opening, and journal starvation.

FORMULA MODELS

MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Deterministic fluid dynamics and non-linear degradation laws governing the calculation workbench.

1. Viscosity Consistency Score (S_visc)

Measures kinematic deviation from nominal SAE factory grade. Both excessive fuel thinning and sludge thickening are penalized exponentially:

S_visc = 100 × exp( -2.8 × |( ν_curr / ν_0 ) - 1.0|^1.5 )

Where ν_curr is measured viscosity (cSt) and ν_0 is nominal grade centerpoint.

2. Optical Transmittance Score (S_trans)

Translates light penetration percentage (T%) measured across a 1mm fluid gap into a normalized optical health factor:

S_trans = ( T / 100 )^0.75 × 100

The 0.75 exponent models non-linear soot particle opacity clustering and light scattering.

3. Composite Oil Health Index (OHI)

Multi-factor composite scoring combining optical soot clarity (45%), kinematic viscosity integrity (40%), and blotter dispersancy (15%):

OHI = 0.45 × S_trans + 0.40 × S_visc + 0.15 × S_blotter

An OHI ≤ 30% triggers an immediate critical service flag to avoid hydrodynamic film collapse.

4. Duty-Cycle Remaining Useful Life (RUL)

Calculates remaining safe running distance before reaching the critical wear cutoff:

RUL_km = max( 0 , D_nominal × M_duty × [(OHI - 25) / 75] )

M_duty incorporates Base Oil Chemistry, Driving Mode, and Engine Architecture factors.
